Privacy Policy

Effective November 22, 2026

Gigglebean helps parents and caregivers record and share baby-care information. This policy explains what information the app handles, why it is needed, and the choices available to you.

Information we collect

Depending on the features you use, Gigglebean may process:

  • Account information, including your name, email address, authentication details, and account identifiers.
  • Baby profile information, such as a name, birth date, profile photo, and growth measurements.
  • Care and wellness records, including feeding, nursing, solids, sleep, diaper, temperature, medicine, vaccination, milestone, journal, reminder, and related notes.
  • Photos you choose to add, including profile and activity photos.
  • Family-sharing information, such as caregiver names, email addresses, roles, invitations, and change requests.
  • Preferences and device data, including notification choices, units, goals, the active profile, and information needed to operate and secure the service.

We do not sell personal information.

How we use information

We use information to:

  • Create and secure your account.
  • Save, synchronize, display, export, and restore the records you enter.
  • Provide summaries, insights, goals, reminders, and notifications.
  • Let family owners share selected baby profiles with authorized caregivers.
  • Maintain app reliability, prevent misuse, and comply with legal obligations.

Storage and service providers

Gigglebean uses Google Firebase services for authentication, cloud data storage, hosting, and related infrastructure. Information may also be stored locally on your device so the app can remember preferences and support its features. These providers process information on our behalf under their own security and privacy commitments.

When information is shared

Information is shared only as needed:

  • With caregivers you authorize, according to the role and baby profile assigned by the family owner.
  • With service providers, such as Firebase, that operate the app’s infrastructure.
  • When required by law, necessary to protect safety or rights, or involved in a business transfer subject to appropriate safeguards.

Caregiver invitation codes should be shared only with people you trust.

Your choices and controls

  • You can review and edit records and baby profiles in the app.
  • You can export a JSON copy of tracker records from the data settings page.
  • Family owners can remove caregivers, revoke invitations, and delete baby profiles.
  • You can manage notification permission in the app and in your device settings.
  • You can delete your account from Settings. See our account deletion instructions or contact us for assistance.

Retention and deletion

We retain account and family information while your account is active and as needed to provide Gigglebean. When you delete a baby profile or account, the app initiates deletion of the associated information from active systems. Limited copies may remain temporarily in provider backups, security records, or where retention is legally required.

Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, including authenticated access and access controls. No storage or transmission method is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Use a strong password and protect caregiver invitation codes.

Children’s privacy

Gigglebean is designed for parents and adult caregivers, not for use by children. Adults provide and control information about babies and children in their care. If you believe a child has provided account information directly without appropriate consent, contact us so we can investigate and delete it.

International processing

Our service providers may process information in countries other than the one where you live. Where required, appropriate protections are used for these transfers.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Gigglebean changes. We will post the revised policy here and update its effective date. Material changes may also be communicated in the app or through another appropriate channel.
