Gigglebean

Account and data controls

Delete your Gigglebean account

You can permanently delete your account and associated Gigglebean data from the app, or request help by email.

Delete your account in the app

  1. Sign in to Gigglebean.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Scroll to the account deletion section and select Delete account.
  4. Enter your password to confirm your identity.
  5. Confirm permanent deletion.

If you forgot your password, use the password-reset option before completing these steps.

Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the app, email us from the address associated with your Gigglebean account. Use the subject “Delete my Gigglebean account” and include the account email address in your request. We may ask for additional information to verify that you own the account.

Email gigglebean.app@gmail.com

Data that is deleted

For a family-owner account, deletion removes:

  • Your Gigglebean authentication account and account identifiers.
  • Baby profiles and profile photos owned by the account.
  • Feeding, nursing, solids, sleep, diaper, growth, milestone, journal, reminder, and related records.
  • Uploaded activity photos.
  • Caregiver access records, invitations, and change requests associated with the deleted profiles.

Device-local app data can be removed by uninstalling Gigglebean or clearing the app’s storage after account deletion.

Data that may be kept

Gigglebean does not intentionally keep an application-level copy of deleted account data for an additional retention period. Limited data may remain temporarily in service-provider backups or security records, or when retention is required by law. Backup copies are not used for normal app operation and are removed through the provider’s standard retention cycle.

If you are a caregiver in another person’s family, records owned by that family are controlled by the family owner and are not deleted when your caregiver account is deleted.

Before you delete

Account deletion is permanent. If you want a copy of your tracker records, use Settings → Your records → Download baby data before deleting your account.
